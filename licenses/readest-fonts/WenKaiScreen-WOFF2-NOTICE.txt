LXGW WenKai GB Screen web font

The bundled WOFF2 is a web-font format conversion of the unmodified
LXGWWenKaiGBScreen.ttf version 1.530 by LXGW. It is used by the embedded
reader, and the original TTF is retained as a fallback. The font is licensed
under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see LXGWWenKaiGBScreen-OFL.txt for the
copyright, reserved font names, and license terms.

Source: https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai-Screen
